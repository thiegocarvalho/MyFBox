# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, the maintainers and contributors of **MyFBox**, pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behaviour

- Use welcoming, respectful, and objective language.
- Accept constructive criticism with a technical focus.
- Prioritise what is best for the community and the security of the project's users.
- Show empathy towards other participants.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Derogatory comments, insults, or personal/political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit consent.
- Any conduct incompatible with a professional and collaborative environment.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behaviour and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and Pull Requests that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, as well as temporarily or permanently ban participants in severe or repeated cases.

To report violations, contact the maintainers privately through the GitHub repository channels.
